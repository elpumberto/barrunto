# Changelog

## 2.1.0

A third rule pack, Jetective Jev, and with it two packs on one site.

- **Jetective Jev.** On a profile of X.com, a case file on the account: whether it looks automated, like a scam, a billboard or a farm of reactions, or like a maker, a pro or somebody's own account, how strong the hunch is, and the exhibits for and against. Jev is asked what kind of post each post is and code counts, so an exhibit reads "7 of 12 posts read as adverts". It judges what an account does, never who is behind it; it says *looks like*, never *is*; and none of its labels is noise: nobody is faded or hidden.
- **What it sends.** The name shown, the bio and the words of up to thirty posts the page has shown. The handle, the counts and the dates are weighed by code. It looks again as you go down a profile. Protected accounts are left alone.
- **Two packs on one site.** X Posts, as the pack that labels posts is now called, and Jetective Jev share X.com, each turned on and off by itself. Chrome asks your leave once for the site and gets it back with the last of them. Over X.com the popup names both and shows one at a time.
- **Permissions.** Leave given from Chrome's own pages no longer turns a pack on by itself: the pack's switch does, with no further question.
- **Tuning mode.** Readable with many judgments: the folded line names what matters and counts the rest, and what came to nothing is named in a line.

**Updating from 2.0.0:** nothing to do. Jetective Jev is off until you turn it on in Rule packs.

## 2.0.0

Barrunto is no longer only for X.com: what is specific to a site is now a rule pack, turned on and off in the popup.

- **Rule packs.** X, and a new one for Hacker News comments: Insight, Snark, Tangent.
- **Noise.** Per label, choose whether what gets it is only labelled, faded or hidden.
- **Read ahead.** Items are analyzed a few ahead of you, so labels are there when you arrive.
- **Drafts.** On X, a hunch about your own post as you write it.
- **Popup.** Reorganised around the page it is opened over; lighter tuning mode.
- **Permissions.** Chrome is asked leave for a site only when its pack is turned on.

**Updating from 1.0.0:** your key and settings are kept, but Chrome withdraws its leave over x.com. Open the popup on X and turn the pack on again.
